Getting Around

Choose a way to travel that fits your plans and where you are staying.

Taniti City busesPublic buses run daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m.
Beyond the cityPrivate buses serve the rest of the island; taxis are available in Taniti City.
Drive or rideRental cars are available near the airport. Bikes and helmets are available to rent; helmets are required by law.
Explore on footTaniti City is fairly flat and walkable. Merriton Landing is easy to explore on foot.
Most visitors arrive by air at the small airport. Some arrive on a cruise ship that docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night each week.

Before You Go

Practical details to help you plan a comfortable visit.

Money and outletsTaniti uses the U.S. dollar. Some businesses also accept euros and yen. Outlets are 120 volts.
HealthOne hospital and several clinics serve the island. The hospital has many multilingual employees.
LanguageMany younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents.
SafetyViolent crime is very rare; visitors should be mindful of pickpocketing and other petty crime.
Hours and holidaysAlcohol cannot be served or sold from midnight to 9 a.m. Many attractions and restaurants may close on national holidays.
Drinking ageThe drinking age is 18 and is not strictly enforced.

Plan ahead for holiday closures, and check activity availability before traveling around the island.
